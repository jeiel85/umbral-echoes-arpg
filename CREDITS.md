# CREDITS & LICENSES

## Umbral Echoes: Forsaken Depths
An original 2D Dark Gothic Fantasy Hack & Slash Action RPG.

### Assets & Technology Attributions

- **Game Engine**: [Phaser 3](https://phaser.io/) (MIT License) - Copyright (c) Richard Davey.
- **Development Tooling**: [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) (Apache-2.0), [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/) (MIT License).
- **Graphics & Visuals**:
  - 100% original procedural canvas pixel art generated dynamically in real-time via `TextureGenerator.ts`.
  - Custom Gothic dark fantasy sprites, armor, monsters, boss silhouettes, tilesets, lighting and FX.
- **Audio & Sound Effects**:
  - 100% procedural sound synthesizer utilizing the Web Audio API (`SoundEngine.ts`).
  - Synthesized organic retro ARPG swings, hits, magical casts, fire explosions, frost crunches, lightning bursts, and ambient drone. Zero external audio asset dependencies.
- **Original Lore, Systems & Design**:
  - Original world setting, classes (Vanguard, Arcanist), monsters, bosses, legendary items, and quests designed from scratch.
